# Grounded motorsport visual pass

The starting-grid scene now uses a more realistic, sim-racing-inspired direction: galvanized steel roof trusses, concrete terraces and barriers, paddock buildings, catch fencing, track floodlights, muted asphalt, and natural overcast daylight. The sci-fi neon canopies, halo towers, and luminous spires have been removed.

The eight original procedural open-wheel cars remain interactive and retain their launch animation. Their paint is now a subdued motorsport palette with less glossy materials. This is still a lightweight procedural 3D scene rather than licensed Assetto Corsa assets or photorealistic scanned vehicles.

The projects carousel and all other sections are carried over from the prior free-scroll version.
