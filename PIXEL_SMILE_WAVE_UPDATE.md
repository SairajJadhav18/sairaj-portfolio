# Pixel: smiling and overhead wave

Pixel now has permanent glowing smiling eyes, a curved mouth, and pink cheeks. Its right arm is raised and waves over its head in the idle animation, with more enthusiastic waves on interaction. Cursor tracking, smooth head movement, meteor shower, and form interactions remain.

The form still opens the visitor's email app. Run `npm install` and `npm run dev`. Production/browser testing has not been completed.
